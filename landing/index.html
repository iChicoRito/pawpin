From a photo on the street to a rescue.

  1. Take a photo

    The place and time are fixed the moment the first photo is taken, so the pin is where the animal was.

  2. Say how urgent it is

    Pick the animal, its size, color, and condition, then one of three urgency levels. Add a landmark if it helps.

  3. A rescuer answers

    Someone nearby taps I’m on my way. One rescuer per report, so two people do not make the same trip.

  4. It ends with an outcome

    Rescued, or not found. The person who reported it is told either way.

What is in the app.

Each pin says how urgent.

  • CriticalBadly hurt or in danger right now.
  • Needs help soonHurt, sick, or weak, but not in danger right now.

Stay safe on the way there.

The app carries safety tips for going near an animal you do not know. Four of them:

  • Keep your distance at firstWatch it before you go near. A scared or hurt animal may bite.
  • Move slowly and stay lowNo sudden moves or loud voice. Do not stare or stand over it.
  • Do not corner itLeave it a way out. An animal with no escape defends itself.
  • Protect your handsUse a towel, a blanket, or thick gloves if you must touch it.

Installing it

  1. Tap Download APK on your Android phone.
  2. Open the file. If Android asks, allow your browser to install apps.
  3. Tap Install, then open PawPin.

What it asks for

  • Camera, to photograph the stray you are reporting.
  • Location, so rescuers can find the animal. Only while the app is open.
  • Notifications, for alerts about strays near you. You can skip this.

There is no iPhone version.
